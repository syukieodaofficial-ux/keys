Public Service Profile

KC Grace O. Bandoy is a passionate youth leader serving as the elected Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) Chairperson of Barangay Centro, Sibulan, located in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur, Philippines.

Concurrently, KC is pursuing her ongoing tertiary education as a Bachelor of Secondary Education student majoring in English (Ongoing). As an aspiring educator, she deeply believes that quality education and youth empowerment are the cornerstone of a vibrant, progressive community.

Her public-service focus centers on youth participation, educational assistance, community development, skills building, culture and arts, and fostering open community engagement. Through her role in the Sangguniang Kabataan, she actively collaborates with youth council members and barangay officials to deliver transparent public service.

The Role of the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK)

In the Philippine local governance framework, the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) serves as the official youth council in every barangay, advocating for youth development, health, education, environmental protection, sports, culture, and active civic participation.

As SK Chairperson of Barangay Centro, Sibulan, Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur, KC Grace O. Bandoy works to represent the youth in the Barangay Council, ensuring young constituents have a voice in local decision-making.

Key Public Service Focus Areas

Youth Participation

Creating opportunities for young citizens to participate in barangay meetings, leadership workshops, and civic programs.

Educational Support

Promoting educational assistance programs and encouraging youth retention in schools across Davao del Sur.

Community Improvement

Leading practical barangay projects such as the installation of clear purok signages in Barangay Sibulan.
